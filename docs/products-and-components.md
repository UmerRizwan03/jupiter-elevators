# Elevator Spare Parts & Components Catalog Structure

This document outlines the complete spare parts and components catalog for **Jupiter Elevators**. It serves as the baseline data architecture for the website catalog, filtering systems, and RFQ workflows.

---

## 1. Catalog Hierarchy & Categories

### Category 1: Elevator Controllers (لوحات التحكم)
* **Main Control Boards (لوحات التحكم الرئيسية):** Integrated microprocessor controllers, serial communication mainboards, car top boards, and landing boards compatible with standard traction and hydraulic lifts.
* **VVVF Inverter Drives (محولات التردد المتغير):** Energy-efficient variable frequency drives designed for elevator passenger travel smoothness, floor leveling accuracy, and silent braking.
* **Relays & Contactors (المرحلات والمفاتيح الكهرومغناطيسية):** Heavy-duty safety contactors, auxiliary relays, phase sequence relays, and motor starters.
* **Transformers & Power Supplies (المحولات ووحدات إمداد الطاقة):** Step-down isolation transformers, regulated DC power supplies, and emergency battery rescue units (ARD / UPS).

### Category 2: Traction Machine & Drive Systems (ماكينات الجر ونظم الدفع)
* **Gearless & Geared Traction Machines (ماكينات بدون تروس ومع تروس):** High-torque permanent magnet synchronous motors (PMSM) and robust worm-geared drive units.
* **Traction Sheaves (طارات الجر):** Precision-grooved cast iron traction sheaves, deflector sheaves, and secondary diverter pulleys.
* **Brakes & Brake Coils (الفرامل وملفات الفرامل):** Electromagnetic dual-shoe brakes, release levers, brake pads, and heavy-duty actuation coils.
* **Bearings & Encoders (المحامل وأجهزة التشفير):** High-speed rotary incremental and absolute encoders, shielded bearing assemblies for smooth shaft rotation.

### Category 3: Guide Rails & Fixing Accessories (سكك التوجيه وملحقات التثبيت)
* **Machined & Cold-Drawn Guide Rails (سكك التوجيه):** Standard industry profiles: T50 / T70 / T75 / T78 / T89 / T90 rails for cabins and counterweights.
* **Rail Clips & Brackets (مشابك وحوامل السكك):** Forged steel sliding and rigid rail clips, adjustable wall brackets, and alignment hardware.
* **Fishplates & Fasteners (ألواح التوصيل ومسامير التثبيت):** Precision-milled connecting fishplates, high-tensile connection bolts, and anchor bolts.
* **Guide Rail Lubricators (مزيتات سكك التوجيه):** Automatic felt and wick lubricators, oil reservoirs, and environmentally safe collector trays.

### Category 4: Door Operators & Mechanisms (مشغلات الأبواب)
* **Automatic Door Operators (مشغلات الأبواب الأوتوماتيكية):** Telescopic, center-opening, and side-opening car door driving mechanisms.
* **VVVF Door Operators (مشغلات أبواب بتحكم متغير السرعة):** High-efficiency smart door operators with integrated frequency inverters, obstacle detection, and speed profiling.
* **Door Motors & Controllers (محركات ووحدات تحكم الأبواب):** DC brushless and PM synchronous door drive motors, encoder feedback modules, and dedicated controller cards.
* **Rollers, Belts & Linkages (بكرات وسير وأذرع التوصيل):** Polyurethane hanger rollers, high-torque toothed sync belts, linkage arms, and emergency release cords.

### Category 5: Landing Doors & Assemblies (أبواب الأدوار وملحقاتها)
* **Complete Landing Door Assemblies (مجموعات أبواب الأدوار الكاملة):** Certified fire-rated landing door frames, headers, sills, and panel mechanisms.
* **Door Panels & Frames (درف وإطارات الأبواب):** Stainless steel (hairline/mirror/etched), powder-coated steel, and architectural glass door panels.
* **Door Locks & Interlocks (أقفال وكوالين الأبواب الكهربائية):** EN 81 compliant electromechanical landing door interlocks, hook switches, and safety contact bridges.
* **Door Sills & Tracks (أعتاب ومجاري الأبواب):** Extruded heavy-duty aluminum and steel bottom sills, hanging guide tracks, and anti-vibration shoe guides.

### Category 6: Push Buttons & Indicators (أزرار الطلب والمؤشرات)
* **COP & LOP Push Buttons (أزرار لوحات الكابينة والأدوار):** Vandal-resistant stainless steel buttons, micro-motion switches, dual-color LED rings, and touch buttons.
* **Braille & ADA-Compliant Buttons (أزرار بطريقة برايل):** Embossed Braille floor designations, tactile numerals, and audible acknowledgment tones.
* **Floor & Direction Indicators (شاشات عرض الأدوار والاتجاهات):** Segment LED, dot-matrix, and full-color multimedia TFT/LCD displays.
* **Hall Lamps & Arrival Buzzers (مصابيح الأدوار وأجراس التنبيه):** Directional arrival lanterns, electronic chimes, emergency alarm bells, and intercom stations.

### Category 7: Guide Shoes & Inserts (كراسي التوجيه والبطانات)
* **Sliding & Roller Guide Shoes (كراسي التوجيه المنزلقة والدوارة):** Heavy-duty cast iron sliding shoes, spring-loaded high-speed polyurethane roller assemblies.
* **Adjustable Guide Shoes (كراسي توجيه قابلة للتعديل):** Precision threaded adjustment shoes for perfect plumb rail alignment.
* **Counterweight Shoes (كراسي ثقل الموازنة):** Compact sliding and roller guides engineered for high counterweight travel stability.
* **Inserts & Wear Liners (بطانات كراسي التوجيه):** Self-lubricating nylon, PTFE, and low-friction composite replacement liners.

### Category 8: Safety Gear & Governors (منظومة الأمان ومنظم السرعة)
* **Overspeed Governors (منظمات السرعة الزائدة):** Bi-directional and uni-directional centrifugal governors for passenger and freight speeds.
* **Safety Gear Systems (أجهزة الأمان ومكابح الطوارئ):** Progressive safety gears (for high-speed elevators) and instantaneous safety gears (for low-speed/hydraulic lifts).
* **Governor Ropes & Tension Pulleys (حبال وبكرات شد منظم السرعة):** Steel wire ropes and weighted pit tension pulleys with electrical safety break switches.
* **Safety Interlock Switches (مفاتيح الأمان الكهربائية):** Slack rope safety switches, pit stop switches, and emergency escape hatch contacts.

### Category 9: Wire Ropes & Suspension Equipment (حبال الجر ومعدات التعليق)
* **Steel Wire Ropes (حبال الجر الفولاذية):** High-grade elevator steel wire ropes (8x19S, 8x19W, 6x19), fiber core (FC) and independent wire rope core (IWRC).
* **Governor & Speed Limiter Ropes (حبال منظم السرعة):** Small-diameter flexible steel cables with wear-resistant finishes.
* **Rope Sockets & Clamps (مربط الحبال والمشابك):** Wedge sockets, babbit sockets, and drop-forged heavy duty rope clamps.
* **Compensation Chains & Ropes (سلاسل وأحبال التعويض):** Plastic-coated quiet compensation chains, damping pulleys, and balance weights for high-rise shafts.

### Category 10: Sensors & Door Safety (حساسات السلامة وأجهزة الاستشعار)
* **Light Curtains & Photocells (الستائر الضوئية والخلايا الكهروضوئية):** Multi-beam infrared light curtains, full door-height safety curtains, and single-beam safety eyes.
* **Limit & Deceleration Switches (مفاتيح نهاية الشوط والتخفيض):** Bi-stable magnetic switches, roller mechanical limit switches, and terminal safety cutoffs.
* **Proximity & Leveling Sensors (حساسات التقارب وضبط التوقف):** Inductive proximity sensors, vane switches, and magnetic tape reader leveling heads.
* **Door Contacts & Safety Edges (نقاط تلامس الأبواب وحواف الأمان):** Spring-loaded safety contacts, mechanical bumper edges, and micro-switch assemblies.

### Category 11: Hydraulic Elevator Components (مكونات المصاعد الهيدروليكية)
* **Pumps & Hydraulic Power Units (مضخات ووحدات الطاقة الهيدروليكية):** Submerged screw pumps, AC power packs, and hydraulic oil tanks.
* **Valves & Solenoids (صمامات التحكم والملفات اللولبية):** Proportional control valves, emergency lowering valves, check valves, and pressure relief solenoids.
* **Cylinders & High-Pressure Hoses (أسطوانات وخراطيم الضغط العالي):** Single and telescopic hydraulic rams, rupture safety valves, and steel braided flexible hoses.
* **Seals & Oil Filters (موانع التسريب وفلاتر الزيت):** High-pressure polyurethane piston seals, rod packings, return line filters, and breather caps.

### Category 12: Other Key Components & Shaft Equipment (مكونات إضافية وتجهيزات البئر)
* **Buffers & Pit Equipment (المصدات وتجهيزات قاع البئر):** Hydraulic oil buffers, polyurethane and spring buffers, pit ladders, and pit inspection control boxes.
* **Cabin Fans & Ventilation (مراوح وتهوية الكابينة):** Cross-flow low-noise cabin blowers, axial exhaust fans, and LED energy-saving shaft lighting.
* **Handrails & Architectural Mirrors (درابزينات ومرايا الكابينة):** Stainless steel safety handrails (round/flat/curved), safety laminated mirrors, and car bumper rails.
* **Fasteners & Installation Accessories (مثبتات ومستلزمات التركيب):** High-grade anchor bolts, expansion screws, vibration dampeners, and shims.

## 2. Technical Data Schema for Website Products

Each product in the database or JSON data store adheres to the following unified schema:

```typescript
export interface ElevatorPart {
  id: string;                         // e.g. "ctrl-main-board-01"
  sku: string;                        // e.g. "JP-CB-204"
  slug: string;                       // e.g. "universal-integrated-controller-board"
  categoryId: string;                 // e.g. "elevator-controllers"
  subcategory: string;                // e.g. "Main Control Boards"
  name: {
    en: string;                       // e.g. "Universal Integrated Controller Board"
    ar: string;                       // e.g. "لوحة تحكم رئيسية متكاملة لجميع المصاعد"
  };
  description: {
    en: string;
    ar: string;
  };
  specifications: Record<string, string>; // e.g. { "Voltage": "AC 220V", "Floors": "Up to 32" }
  compatibleBrands: string[];         // e.g. ["Monarch", "Step", "Otis", "Schindler", "Universal"]
  inStock: boolean;                   // true (Ready in Saudi Arabia)
  origin: "China" | "India" | "International";
  images: string[];                   // URLs/paths to product images
  datasheetUrl?: string;              // Optional PDF link
}
```
