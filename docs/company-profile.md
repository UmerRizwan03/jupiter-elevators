## 3. Business Overview & Value Proposition
### What Jupiter Elevators Does
Jupiter Elevators specializes in the supply and distribution of elevator spare parts, components, and technical replacement solutions. The company imports high-quality components directly from verified manufacturers in India and China, delivering reliable parts to lift manufacturers, maintenance firms, MEP contractors, and certified technicians across Saudi Arabia.

### Why Jupiter Elevators Was Founded
To address a major pain point in the Saudi Arabian elevator industry: **frequent spare parts shortages, high downtime, and lengthy international procurement lead times**. By concentrating strictly on spare parts inventory rather than complete lift packages, Jupiter Elevators ensures ready stock, rapid local delivery, and competitive pricing.

### Core Strengths & Heritage
1. **Unrivaled Industry Experience:** Backed by industry veterans with **30+ years of direct elevator industry experience** and **33+ years of import, logistics, and supply chain warehousing expertise**.
2. **Direct Manufacturer Partnerships:** Strategic collaborations with established, certified elevator component manufacturers in China and India.
3. **Comprehensive In-Stock Focus:** Keeping high-wear and critical elevator components readily available in Saudi Arabia.
4. **End-to-End Technical Support:** Beyond part sales, offering component identification, technical consulting, and emergency assistance.

## 5. Scope of Services
1. **Elevator Spare Parts & Component Sourcing:** Rapid procurement of standard and rare replacement parts from top-tier Indian and Chinese manufacturing hubs.
2. **Technical Support & Part Identification:** Assisting engineers and technicians in identifying obsolete, worn, or unbranded parts from photos and technical specs.
3. **Preventive & Corrective Maintenance Support:** Supporting maintenance teams with scheduled replacement packages and wear-and-tear components.
4. **Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) Support:** Component supply agreements for facility managers and elevator contractors with pre-negotiated delivery schedules.
5. **Emergency Breakdown Assistance:** Priority dispatch and expedited component sourcing to minimize building elevator downtime.
6. **Technical Consultation & Modernization Advice:** Advising on controller upgrades, door operator modernizations, and energy-efficient drive replacements.
